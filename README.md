## What I'm working on

Since April 2026 I lead technology at **Retenlins**, a Brazilian manufacturer with 20+ years in heat exchangers, seals and water treatment, serving large food and beverage companies. It is the company's first tech team, and the mission is to make the commercial operation run on data, not just get a new system.

- **IARA**, an AI agent on WhatsApp that became the sales team's main channel to log visits, contacts and follow-ups in the CRM, by voice or text. Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, tool calling, hybrid RAG (pgvector + BM25), human confirmation before anything is written, and LangSmith for cost, latency and regressions.
- **An in-house CRM**, built slice by slice from interviews with the sales team and gradually replacing the legacy ERP. ASP.NET Core / .NET 10, PostgreSQL and Next.js.
- **An AI SDR** for outbound prospecting. In the August 2026 pilot it reached 144 contacts, 59 replied and 10 reached a positive outcome.
- **Pricing** moved from Excel/VBA to a C# engine, checked against the spreadsheet on real cases with the operations team.

What I measure: adoption by real actions (writes, not logins). CRM actions grew 4x in three weeks, and quote data completeness went from ~56% to nearly 100% by bringing the fields to where the team already worked.

## Public projects

LangGraph with human-in-the-loop approval, FastAPI, offline evals in LangSmith, mypy strict. Every line written by hand.

Most of my production work lives in private company repositories.
